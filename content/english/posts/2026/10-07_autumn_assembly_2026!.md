Hello, all Computer Scientists, it’s almost time for this year’s autumn assembly!
But what is an autumn assembly?
The autumn assembly is one of the Uppsala Computer Science Section’s two annual general assemblies, to which all section members are invited! At the autumn assembly, the section’s new board is elected to serve for the coming year, and elections are held to replace the section’s representatives on UTN and the program council.
The positions to be filled at this assembly are as follows:
Chair
Vice Chair
Secretary
Chair of the Student Council
Head of Events
Asset Manager
Head of Information
Study Liaison Officer
Head of Business Relations
Auditor
Representative to the program council for the bachelor’s Programme in Computer Science
Representative to the program council for the master's Programme in Computer Science
Representative to the Department of Information Technology
Representative to the UTN union council
Representative to UTN’s Nomination Committee
Convener of DV’s nomination committee
Would you like to run for a position yourself or nominate someone?

https://datavetenskap.nu/documents/Reglemente.pdf
If you don’t want to run for a position, why should you attend the autumn assembly?
In addition to having a say in who will represent the section, the general meeting is also the perfect opportunity to make your voice heard and gives you the chance to influence what our section will focus on in the coming year.
Before the meeting, you can submit motions for things you think should be introduced or changed!
Here’s a template for how to write a motion:
https://docs.google.com/.../1qFXpYFlQlbsiPqNYMHbv.../
Email your motions to dv-ordf@utn.se!
(NOTE! Motions for the autumn assembly must be submitted no later than October 23rd at 11:59 p.m.)
